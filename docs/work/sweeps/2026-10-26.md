## 1. Top: the new host files split `main.ts` into pieces that still share all of its state

- **Evidence:** bundle, `src/main.ts` 531 lines (-5119), and six new files: `page-builder-host.ts`
  (794), `pages-and-files-host.ts` (754), `workspace-mount.ts` (662), `site-loading.ts` (566),
  `workspace-boot.ts` (496), `setup-host.ts` (422). `PageBuilderHostPorts` has about 45 members
  (`src/page-builder-host.ts:59-105`), the pages-and-files ports about 65
  (`src/pages-and-files-host.ts:68-139`), several of them other hosts' outputs. `fileGeneration` goes into
  six hosts as a get/set pair (`src/main.ts:236, 376, 389, 450, 470`); `nativePreview`, `pageStructure` and
  `componentTools` are set from inside the mount (`src/workspace-mount.ts:428, 494, 561, 583`).
  `page-builder-host.ts:46-51` imports types from three other hosts, which take its outputs back: a cycle the
  getters hide. Tests pin the plumbing ("…through its setters", `tests/workspace-mount.test.ts:261, 331,
  353`). `tests/main-ts-ceiling.test.ts:6` caps `main.ts` at 540 lines, which rewards moving lines out
  rather than deepening. Deletion test: inlining any host just moves its code back. `site-loading.ts`
  and `setup-host.ts` import none of the others and are the exception.
- **Fix:** give the open workspace's shared state one owner module that the hosts depend on, instead of
  50-odd loose ports; then swap the line ceiling for a check on cross-host imports and port count.

  `src/page-builder/rules/*.ts` already are; start with the job the next ticket touches.

## 3. Top: the kit's hand-made file lock was found broken five times in one week

- **Evidence:** review findings, kind `race`, all on the record lock in `scripts/agents/kit-lib.mjs`:
  no lock at all (38e24241 :283), a lock that is never treated as stale after a crash (310808b7 :292),
  then three different ways the stale-lock takeover lets two writers in (ed0364de :323, 1331c210 :321,
  36961d73 :312). Each fix opened the next hole.
- **Fix:** replace the hand-made lock with one the system releases when the process dies (`flock` on
  the record file, or one small locking library), in one shared helper with a two-writer test.
  **Guardrail candidate:** a lint rule (`no-restricted-syntax` on `mkdirSync`/`renameSync` of `*.lock`
  paths in `scripts/agents/**`) so new locks go through that helper.

## 5. A boot-memory browser test passed only on retry in two different builds

- **Evidence:** bundle, "Tests that failed then passed": `native-boot-memory.spec.ts` › "a warm reload
  reads the remembered snapshot…" flipped in lead-39 and lead-52. The spec waits for
  `.hero h1[data-guess]` to be visible (`tests/native-save/native-boot-memory.spec.ts:68`) after
  releasing the held reads; if the proven paint lands first, the guess mark may already be gone (likely,
  not confirmed).
- **Fix:** diagnose with the `diagnosing-bugs` loop (repeat the spec 30× under load) and assert on
  something that cannot disappear, such as a recorded "painted from guess" event.
  **Guardrail candidate:** a kit check in `kit pr` (`scripts/agents/kit.d/pr`) that, when a spec has
  flipped in two runs, says so and asks for a `tests/known-failures.json` entry or a fix.

## 7. Date windows in the routine tools are off by a day, three times

- **Evidence:** review findings, kind `race`: `kit learn --since 14d` drops the last run's day
  (7b7aed3, `scripts/agents/kit.d/learn:56`); `kit sweep` picks corrections up to yesterday from
  committed logs (dea5076f9, `scripts/agents/kit.d/sweep:248`); a test computes the day line in its own

  `kit.d/sweep` outside it.
