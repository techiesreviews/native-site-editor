### 1. Status at each milestone, so Lex never has to ask "how are things?" (×6)

- **Evidence:** corrections list (`native-site-editor.md`), chats "Find Open Tasks" and "Remove Media
  Card from Image Edit Bar": 2026-10-21 (line 29, borderline: the agent had been posting progress),
  2026-10-26 three times (lines 14, 15 and 11: twice within about an hour, a third time after the release
  had started), 2026-10-27 twice (line 10 at about 07:03 after a night with no status, line 9 at 09:32).
- **Covered?** Yes, by a steering line, not a check. Lex's global instructions ask for "a two-line status
  (done, running, next) at each milestone, so I never have to ask". `kit learn`'s guess,
  `docs/agents/guardrails.md:19`, is the subagent cap and does not cover it. Why it did not hold: the line
  is a reminder in a long context, and nothing marks the moment a milestone ends. During `kit go` waits a
  lead also sees "never post progress between waits", which is meant for children but reads as a reason
  to stay quiet. No repeat since 2026-10-27, so it may be holding now, though nothing enforces it.
- **Proposed action: hook.** A PostToolUse hook in the shared kit's hooks (`hooks/run.sh` in the
  agent-kit repo, beside the bash guard) that watches lead sessions for a milestone result: a
  `RESULT: ok pr … merged` line, a slice's final RESULT line, or a deploy. It then adds one context line
  asking for the two-line status (done, running, next) before the next tool call. It catches a lead that
  merges or finishes a step and moves on without telling Lex. Children (sessions with a `kit-brief:`
  marker) are skipped, because they report to their lead, not to Lex.
