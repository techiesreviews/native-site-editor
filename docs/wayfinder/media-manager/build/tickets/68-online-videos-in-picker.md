## Done (2026-10-15)

- **Behaviour.**
  - **Picker.** Add → Media's picker (slice 63) has one row under the bar: a field "YouTube or Vimeo address" with its own **Use**. An empty, unsupported or not-one-video address gets a short plain message under the field ("Only YouTube and Vimeo videos can be placed. Paste a YouTube or Vimeo address." / "This address is not one video. Copy the address of the video itself.") and nothing is placed. A good address goes in at the place as the starter's Online video (slice 61's markup through slice 67's `addOnlineVideo`: title and still read once, the still copied and prepared into `media/videos/`), start time kept, with the site's player linked (files created only when missing), the record, and selection, as **one Undo step** ("YouTube video added"). A video whose picture the service does not share is placed as its titled link, with slice 67's "add your own still picture" note. The same picker lists online videos under All and Video; choosing one ("Studio tour · YouTube", **Use video**) places it again as recorded, **never contacting the service** (with or without a still). Narrowed pickers (pictures, background video, picture spots) do not list them.
