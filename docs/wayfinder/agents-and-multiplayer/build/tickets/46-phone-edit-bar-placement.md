## Question for Lex (answered 2026-10-29, see Decided behaviour)

On a phone, when the space above the selected element is full of other content, where should the edit bar go?

- **A. Keep it above (today).** It may cover a line of the element above for as long as the selection lasts. This reverses ticket 43's decided behaviour ("the bar never covers the element above it").
- **B. Scroll the page so the bar gets clear space above or below.** The page moves a little when you select something. Where there's no clear space (a dense page), it falls back to A.
- **C. Dock it to the bottom of the canvas on phones**, like a keyboard toolbar. It never covers page content near the selection, but it sits further from what you selected.

No default: until Lex chooses, the bar stays as it is today, and 43's decision stays open rather than reversed.

Also for Lex: ticket 43 waived its "test fails before the fix" rule for F-bar-01, because there was no app fault (its UX check selected `main`, which has no bar by design). A phone spec now pins the behaviour. Accept the waiver, or say what should fail first.

## Decided behaviour (Lex, 2026-10-29: option C)

"Make it work the same as other word editors on mobile": on a phone the edit bar is a toolbar docked to the bottom of the screen, like the formatting toolbar in Google Docs or Word on mobile.

- **Phone only.** "Phone" is the editor window being phone-wide (the editor's existing `(max-width: 650px)` phone rule), not the 390 px device frame chosen on a desktop. Desktop and tablet widths keep today's placement exactly (above, below or pinned by the selection, `editBarPlacement`), including a 390 px device frame on a 1280 px window.
- **Docked.** On a phone the bar spans the canvas width at the bottom of the screen, clear of the home indicator (safe-area inset), with its controls in one row that scrolls sideways when they don't fit (no wrapping into a tall block). It does not move with the selection or the page scroll. Its "Paragraph" style name chip stays first.
- **Never covers page content.** The bar takes its own room at the bottom of the canvas (the page ends above it and can scroll fully clear of it); nothing of the page sits under it. It stays clear of the "… Undo" strip (`src/announce.ts`) and the phone sheet's handle: neither covers the other.
- **Keyboard.** When the on-screen keyboard is open the bar sits right on top of it and follows it as it opens and closes, tracked with the VisualViewport API (`resize` and `scroll` of `window.visualViewport`). The phone layout must not flip when the keyboard takes height (the phone sheet needs `min-height: 500px`), so the layout viewport stays put: set `interactive-widget=resizes-visual` explicitly in `index.html`'s viewport meta and lift the bar by the keyboard's height from VisualViewport. Without VisualViewport the bar simply stays at the bottom.
- **The selection stays visible.** When a new selection (or the bar's own height change, or the keyboard opening) would leave the selected element hidden behind the bar or keyboard, the page scrolls just enough to bring it into view above the bar, once per selection, never on the user's own scroll. This replaces today's phone "room scroll" (`roomScroll`) on phones; desktop is unchanged.
- **Menus and fields from the bar** (Text size, Link's address, Variants, Ask agent's note) open upward from the docked bar and stay inside the screen above it and above the keyboard.
- Feedback stays on the controls (no toasts); theme tokens and the existing bar components only, no new colours; modern CSS (logical properties, `env(safe-area-inset-bottom, 0px)`, no `100vh`). Builder: load the good-css and emil-mobile-native skills before writing the CSS.
- Ticket 43 closes with this ticket: its deferred rule "the bar never covers the element above it" is met on phones by docking. Append a short dated note to 43's Done saying so (and that Lex accepted the F-bar-01 waiver, lead 2026-10-29), and set 43 `status: closed`.

## Done (2026-10-29)

- **Docked on phones.** On a phone-wide window (the editor's `(max-width: 650px)` phone rule) the edit bar is a toolbar in its own row at the foot of the canvas, under the page frame and above the phone sheet's handle: one row, the name chip ("Paragraph") first, then the groups, scrolled sideways when they don't fit. The frame ends where the bar starts, so no page content sits under it and the page's last element scrolls clear above it. It does not move with the selection or the page scroll, and it stays while its selection is scrolled away. Every control in it is a 44 px touch target (U6), the bar as tall as one; its foot keeps 4 px (plus the safe-area inset) between them and the sheet handle. Desktop and tablet widths, including a 390 px device frame on a 1280 px window, keep the placement by the selection unchanged. `src/components/edit-bar.ts`, `src/components/edit-bar.css`.
- **Keyboard.** `index.html`'s viewport meta now says `interactive-widget=resizes-visual`, so the keyboard leaves the layout put. The bar is raised by `dockLift` from `window.visualViewport` (its `resize` and `scroll`) so it sits right on top of the keyboard, or at the foot of a zoomed-in view, never past that view's top. Without VisualViewport it stays at the canvas foot. Only a height change at the same scale counts as the keyboard: a pinch-zoom (the scale changes) moves the bar to the zoomed view's foot but never scrolls the page. Lifted, the bar drops its foot's room (the handle's gap and the home indicator's), so its controls sit right on the keyboard. On phones the fields in the bar, its popovers and Ask agent's note use 16 px text, so iOS opens the keyboard without zooming the page (a zoom would also count as a pinch). The keyboard's lift is a bottom margin on the bar, so the frame ends above the lifted bar and the page's end (and a selection there) still scrolls clear above it; a pinch-zoomed view, panned all the time, only moves the bar (a translate), so zooming never resizes the page. The lift is measured in whole px from the bar's box, so a fractional layout never flips it back and forth.
- **Selection in view.** A new selection hidden behind the bar, the keyboard or a toast standing on the bar is scrolled into view 8 px above it, 400 ms after it is chosen so a double-click can land, measured again at that moment (`dockScroll`). Later changes (the canvas, the bar or the keyboard changing height, a toast showing) scroll it only if it was in view just before, so they are what hid it; a selection the user scrolled away stays put, and one the user scrolls in the 400 ms before the check is left where they put it. A toast that shows checks at once rather than after a wait, and does not push back a new selection's check, so a new element (a Blocks-rail insert) is clear of its "… added" toast from the toast's first frame. Never on the user's own scroll. A selection taller than the room shows its top under the sticky header, or under the visual viewport's top when that is panned below the frame's top (a zoomed view, iOS keeping a caret in sight). This replaces the phone `roomScroll`, which is gone with its test file `tests/edit-bar-room.test.ts`. Ask agent's note stays by the selection, kept above the bar.
- **Toasts.** Only the toast moves: the docked bar carries `data-toast-avoid`, and the toast places itself above it again whenever the docked bar moves, comes or goes (`edit-bar-dock`, sent only when its place changed, not on every page scroll; `src/components/toast.ts`), measured where it rests, not mid-entrance. A toast that was showing before the bar docked (as after a Blocks-rail insert) rises above it; the bar is never raised over the page for a toast.
- **Menus and fields** from the docked bar (Link's address, Variants, menus) open upward from the bar's top edge, never taller than the room between it and the frame's top (at most 320 px; the content scrolls inside), also with the keyboard open. Text size and Heading level are native selects.

- Spec (P3): 6056efff4:docs/agents/features.md:421 — P3 ticket: Ticket 46's Done says "its Files line adds `src/components/toast.ts` and `index.html`". - Spec (P3): 6056efff4:src/components/edit-bar.ts:532 — P3 ticket: The decided behaviour lists Ask agent's note among the "menus and fields from the bar" that "open upward from the docked bar and stay inside the screen above it and above the keyboard". The build keeps the note beside the selection and only pushes it up so it ends above the bar (`placeNote(..., visibleBottom - 4)`). Nothing checks its top, so a tall note on a selection near the frame's top can still run past the visible top in a panned visual viewport. The Done records this departure, but the Decided behaviour section was never changed to match. Fix: either anchor the note to the bar's top edge the way `placePopover` does, or get Lex's OK and change the Decided behaviour bullet.
- Spec (P3): 6056efff4:src/components/edit-bar.ts:317 — P3 ticket: On the docked bar, `placePopover` limits the popover's height to `top - frameRect.top - 8`, measured up to the frame's top. It ignores a visual viewport that is panned down (`view.offsetTop`, which `dockBar` already works out as `visibleTop`). In a zoomed or panned view, a Link address or Variants popover can therefore reach above the top of the screen. That breaks "stay inside the screen above it". Fix: take the ceiling as `Math.max(frameRect.top, visualViewport.offsetTop)` before working out `maxHeight` and `top`.
- Standards (P2): 6056efff4:src/components/toast.ts:88 — P2 fault: `lift()` passes `innerHeight`, which is the layout viewport, to `toastLift`. Because `interactive-widget=resizes-visual` keeps the layout viewport the same size, an open keyboard leaves the toast at the bottom of the layout viewport (about 790 px at 844), behind the keyboard. The lifted bar sits at roughly 452–500, so `toastLift` finds no overlap and leaves the toast where it is. For the same reason, the "toast standing on the bar" foot in `edit-bar.ts:510`–`527` never applies. Example: someone sets a link in the Address field with the keyboard open, the "… Undo" toast appears behind the keyboard, and its Undo can't be reached. This contradicts the Done's claim that the toast "places itself above it again whenever the docked bar moves". Fix: when the docked bar has `data-lifted`, place the toast against the visual viewport's foot (`visualViewport.offsetTop + height`) instead of `innerHeight`, or treat the keyboard band as an obstacle. Rule: this is a behaviour fault; no CODING_STANDARDS section covers it.
- Standards (P3): 6056efff4:src/components/edit-bar.ts:572 — P3 race: When a new-selection check is still waiting (`roomDue`), a toast opening calls `room(key, { was }, 0)`. That clears the 400 ms timer and scrolls straight away. The 400 ms wait exists so a double-click's second press can land on the element. A background toast that arrives inside that window (a draft save, an agent job) now scrolls the page under the second press, so the second press lands on a different element. Fix: when a toast comes in while `roomDue` is set, keep whatever is left of the 400 ms for pointer-made selections and run at once only for programmatic ones (a Blocks-rail insert). Or hurry the check only when the selection is actually under the toast. Rule: CODING_STANDARDS "Racing and speculative reads" says every outcome must be settled by design; this order (toast arrives during the double-click wait) has no stated outcome.
