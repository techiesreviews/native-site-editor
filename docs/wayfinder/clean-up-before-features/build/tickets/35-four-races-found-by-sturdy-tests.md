## Goal

Factory slice 16 (PR #152) made the load-sensitive specs reliable without changing product code, and found four real product races (its ticket's "For the lead" list). Lex's rule: real faults get fixed.

## Decided behaviour

1. **Audio stops on a render:** a page render while the edit bar's Play runs resets the playing audio's address and stops it, while the bar keeps saying Stop (seen when the code editor arrives late; `src/components/native-preview-runtime.js`, media play and render code). A render keeps a playing audio playing; the bar always matches.
2. **Page jumps mid-drag:** after typing in a text, scrolling away and pressing another block to drag it, the render that writes the typed text scrolls the typed element back into view (`scrollIntoView` nearest), so the page jumps mid-drag (seen: scroll 220 to 89). A render never scrolls while a press or drag is under way, and never scrolls back to text Lex has left.
3. **Explorer hover race** (from factory ticket 14): a pointer on the explorer button opens it by hover, and the click then reopens it. One press opens it once.
4. **Connect to MCP retry race:** a second Connect to MCP press read the connections and opened a socket but sent no `POST /api/agent/connect`; the socket reconnected with a new generation about 2.6 s later. A press always connects exactly once, or shows its problem on the button.

## Done (2026-10-24)

Each race has a spec that failed on the old product code (all four run once with the fixes taken out) and passes with them.

1. **Audio keeps playing through a render.** A render no longer copies the page's `src` and `preload` onto the video or audio that Play is running (a new address reloaded it, paused); the render's address is kept for Stop instead (`syncAttrs`, `src/components/native-preview-runtime.js`). The skipped test in `native-placed-media.spec.ts` is now "a render while the audio plays keeps it playing, the bar saying Stop": it counts the renders the canvas receives and checks one arrived (the code editor's late arrival), the audio still plays from Play's own `blob:` address with the bar on Stop, and Stop then stops it and gives back the page's address (read as its file, not Play's). A second run of the same test plays the audio written with a `<source>` and checks that Stop leaves it no `src`. Before: audio paused, bar on Stop. A render that changes the playing audio's address still stops it (the editor's Play belongs to the element chosen, `src/page-builder/media-play.ts`), and the stop then gives the audio the new address: "an audio whose address changes while it plays stops on the new file, not the one it played" changes Tour's `src` to the Talk file during Play and expects the Talk file's canvas address and length after. Before (the kept address taken out): back on Tour's own. "a render while the video plays keeps it playing and loading; Stop gives it back its still" covers a video: `preload` stays `auto` through a render, and Stop gives back its own `src` and `preload="none"`.
2. **No jump mid-drag.** The render that answers a written text no longer scrolls the selected element into view while a press or drag is under way, nor when it is the text just left (`apply`, the `selectNode` scroll): none of its three ways into view runs then, the plain one, the centred one (redo) and Edit component mode's (below the site's sticky header). The skipped click-to-type test now runs and also checks after the drop that the heading stays out of view and the scroll holds. Before: scroll 220, then 89. A new test, "typing in a text, scrolling it out of view and leaving the canvas: the render that writes it keeps the scroll", covers the text-just-left rule alone: focus leaves the heading with no press, the render that writes it is held, released and handled, and the heading stays out of view. Before (that rule taken out): the heading scrolled back into view. "in Edit component mode, typing in a part scrolled out of view, a press on another part keeps the scroll" types in a card's note, scrolls it just below the frame (the card's body made taller in its CSS for room), holds the render a press on the card's title asks for, releases it, and expects the scroll unchanged before the drop. Before (Edit component mode's reveal not held): the page scrolled to the note.
3. **Explorer opens once.** Every dropdown trigger is now the panel's native invoker (as the secondary trigger already was), so a press on a trigger whose hover opened the panel no longer light-dismisses it before the click; the click handler prevents the native toggle (`src/components/dropdown.ts`; the explorer, Publish, repository, Used on and component-notes menus). A pointer click on an open, pinned panel's trigger keeps it open, as before (the old close-then-reopen); Enter or Space still closes it. The skipped editor-toast test now runs and also checks that the click causes no close or open of the explorer. Before: closed, then opened again over the toast. A new test in `native-branch-menu.spec.ts`, "a pointer click on the open repository menu's trigger keeps it open, with no close and reopen; Enter on it closes it", covers the pointer and keyboard split.
4. **Connect to MCP connects once.** Cause, from slice 33's trace: the refused prompt (a 500) is reported to the live socket, which schedules a reconnect; a press while that reconnect is under way stops it, and the new connection inherits its attempt from browser storage, so it waits out a backoff (about 2.6 s) as "reconnecting" and `turnOn` neither started nor copied. A press that finds the connection reconnecting now retries it at once (as Retry does) and then makes its prompt (`turnOn`, `src/components/agent-menu.ts`); a recorded retry time still disables the button. `turnOn` keeps its activation from the start and stops after each wait (reading the connections, opening the socket, the retry) once a turn-off, another turn-on or a site move has replaced it. New test in `native-mcp-menu.spec.ts`: "a press while the failed prompt's reconnect is under way makes one prompt at once" holds the reconnect's socket, stops the page's clock, presses again, and expects Copied with exactly one `POST /api/agent/connect`; with time stopped, a press that only waited out the backoff never prompts. Before (the retry taken out): stayed on Connect to MCP, no request. A press that still finds the connection reconnecting after the retry (another tab of this browser holds the attempt, or it failed again) made no prompt and now shows the problem dot until the next press: "a press while another tab of this browser holds the reconnect makes no prompt and shows the problem dot" records another tab's live claim in the site's recovery record. Before: no prompt and no problem shown.
