Image popup, Media popup and large image editor, rebuilt from catalogue parts mock-up for Tuesday 10 Nov

The Image popup opens from the edit bar's Media button on a picture: you pick its shape, move the focus square, zoom, turn or replace it, and the large editor gives room to straighten and flip. The Media popup does the same job for a video. Nothing they do changes; every change shows on the page at once and Close keeps it. After shows the same screens built only from the approved parts.

Today: the three screens as they are now, copied from the editor's stylesheets.

Same colours, type and layout. 11 button styles of their own become 3 shared parts, icon buttons in 3 sizes (32, 36 and 56) become 1, 10 corner sizes become the 5 shared ones, about 30 spacing values snap to the scale, and the two shape pickers become one button.
