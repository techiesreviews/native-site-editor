Request pins, rebuilt from catalogue parts mock-up for Tuesday 10 Nov

When you ask an agent about something on the page, a small numbered pin stands on it. The pin shows what the agent is doing, turns orange when it asks you something, and opens a small conversation where you answer. Requests on other pages wait in a pill in the corner. Nothing they do changes: After builds them from the shared catalogue parts. Switch between Today and After, then play the steps.

Today: the pins and their card as the editor draws them now.

Same colours and words. Its own timings (5 durations, 5 curves) give way to the shared motion values: the pop has no bounce and nothing eases in. 2 spinners become 1, the 3 text links in the card become the quiet button, the answer box becomes the shared field with its error line. Hover reacts to a mouse only (4 rules), pins answer a press, and on phones a pin's tap area is 44 px (22 today).
